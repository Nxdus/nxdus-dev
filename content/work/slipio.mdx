Slipio reads Thai bank transfer slips. Upload a batch of slip images, review the extracted details, and export transactions to Google Sheets. You can also save the slip images to Google Drive.

## Why

I built Slipio to reduce manual entry for shops that track payments in spreadsheets. It reads each slip and gives a person a chance to correct the results before export.

## What I built

- **OCR pipeline.** The Next.js frontend uploads slips to a NestJS API, which forwards them to a separate PaddleOCR service.
- **Review step.** Check and correct extracted fields before export.
- **Google integration.** Link one or more spreadsheets, export reviewed transactions, and choose a Google Drive folder for the slip images.

## Technical challenges

**Requesting Google permissions.** Slipio requests Google Sheets access to export transactions and per-file Google Drive access to save slip images. OAuth tokens are stored in an encrypted, HttpOnly session cookie and read by server-side code.

**Restoring settings across devices.** Slipio stores linked spreadsheets and export settings in PostgreSQL under the user's Google account ID. Signing in on another device restores those settings.

**Keeping accounts separate.** Automated tests check that exports use the spreadsheets linked to the signed-in account.
