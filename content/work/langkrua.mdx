Langkrua is a LINE app for small food businesses. Owners track ingredient stock, calculate recipe costs, set menu prices, and record daily orders.

## Why

I built Langkrua to help small food businesses use ingredient costs when setting menu prices. Owners and staff can open the shared workspace from LINE.

## What I built

- **Inventory.** Track stock in the units you use, set prices by package, and open ingredient records from low-stock alerts in LINE.
- **Recipe costing.** Calculate costs from linked ingredients, gas or electricity, and batch yield. Set prices with a target margin and delivery-app commission in mind.
- **Orders and reports.** Record orders and see revenue, costs, commission, and profit by day, week, month, or a custom date range.
- **Shared kitchens.** Staff join a workspace with a six-character code. Owners choose which areas staff can view or manage.
- **Subscriptions.** Stripe handles billing, and each plan comes with its own feature limits.

## Technical challenges

**Working inside LINE.** The mobile-first app runs in Thai through LINE LIFF. Users sign in with their LINE account.

**Keeping shared kitchens in sync.** The app checks for updates every few seconds and holds incoming updates while there are unsaved edits in the same workspace. When you save, the server checks record revisions for conflicts.

**Making join codes easy to share.** Codes omit look-alike characters such as `0` and `O` so staff can share them aloud or in chat.
