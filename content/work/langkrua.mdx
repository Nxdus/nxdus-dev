Langkrua is a back-of-house app for small food businesses. Owners track ingredient stock, cost their recipes, price their menu and record daily orders, all inside LINE.

## Why

Small kitchens price dishes by feel and find out about thin margins at the end of the month. Most of them already run their business through LINE, so the app lives there instead of asking them to install something new.

## What I built

- **Inventory** with units, package-based pricing and low-stock alerts sent as LINE messages that link straight to the ingredient.
- **Recipe costing** from linked ingredients, including gas or electricity cost, batch yield, target margin and delivery-app commission.
- **Daily orders and reports:** revenue, cost, commission and profit by day, week, month or a custom range.
- **Shared kitchens:** an owner creates a workspace, staff join with a six-character code, and the owner sets view or manage access per area.
- **Subscription plans** with Stripe billing and per-plan feature limits.

## Hard parts

**Working well in a mobile LINE browser.** The app is mobile-first and in Thai, runs as a LINE LIFF app, and uses the LINE profile as the identity, so there are no passwords to forget.

**Sync without a real-time server.** Shared kitchens save changes after a short debounce and poll a sync version every few seconds. Remote updates are only applied when there are no unsaved local edits, so two people editing at once don't overwrite each other's work mid-typing.

**Join codes people can read aloud.** Codes avoid characters that are easy to mix up, like `0` and `O`, because they get shared by voice and in chat.
